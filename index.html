Welcome

Researching how digital platforms make better operational decisions.

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, working with Tony Ke. My research sits at the intersection of operations management, digital marketplaces, and technology-enabled service systems.

I study on-demand delivery, platform fulfillment, AI-generated information, and online advertising. My work combines analytical modeling with data-driven insights to understand how new technologies reshape consumer behavior and platform operations.
