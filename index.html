About me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, working with Prof. Tony Ke.

Prior to joining CUHK, I studied Management Science and Engineering at the University of Science and Technology of China under the supervision of Prof. Feng Yang. During my Ph.D., I was very fortunate to be advised by Prof. Fei Gao and Prof. Wenchang Zhang from the Department of Operations and Decision Technologies at the Kelley School of Business, Indiana University. I was also a visiting scholar at the University of Toronto and a visiting student at Indiana University Bloomington.

My research interests include platform operations, on-demand delivery, digital marketplaces, information design, and online advertising.
